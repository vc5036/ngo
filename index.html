Who we are

Working Together for a Better Tomorrow

Asha Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation that began in 2015 with a single evening class for children in one neighbourhood. Today we work alongside families in more than fifteen communities.

Many families we meet are not short of effort. They are short of access: to a school that stays open, a clinic within reach, or a first loan to start a small business. We exist to close those gaps.

We listen first, build with local people, and hand over each programme to the community once it can stand on its own. Our vision is a society where nobody is left behind because of where they were born.

One Opportunity Can Change an Entire Life

Sunita, 34, lives with her three children in a village where most girls leave school by thirteen. Her family's income depended on irregular farm labour.

  • Challenge No steady income and no money for school fees.
  • What we did Tailoring training, a starter loan and a place in a women's savings group.
  • Result Sunita now runs a small tailoring unit, employs two women, and all three children are in school.
